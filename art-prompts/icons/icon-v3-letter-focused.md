## Sora / ChatGPT / Midjourney Prompt

```
App icon design for POURLE casual mobile puzzle game, square 1024×1024,
smooth 3D toon render Royal Match style. Letter-focused icon emphasizing
the Wordle wordplay mechanic.

Composition: 4 large colored liquid letter tiles arranged in 2×2 grid
spelling "BREW" diagonally:
- Top-left: Fire red #E63946 tile with white "B"
- Top-right: Water blue #1D7DBF tile with white "R"
- Bottom-left: Earth green #5C8A3C tile with white "E"
- Bottom-right: Air gold #F4D35E tile with white "W"

Each tile has subtle 3D depth (toon-style, NOT photoreal), soft rounded
corners, slight inner gradient suggesting liquid layer. Small letter
floats slightly above tile surface (3D pop).

Behind the 2×2 grid: Subtle hint of glass apothecary bottle silhouette,
ghosted at 30% opacity, suggesting "these letters come from bottles".
Soft golden glow radiating outward from center.

Background: Warm gradient cream #F5E8D0 → amber #D4B87A. Subtle radial
vignette focusing on letter grid.

Style: Royal Match smooth 3D toon render. Letters MUST be readable at
60×60 px scale — that's the killer test. Each letter big and bold,
high contrast against tile color.

Visual mood: "Wordle but with colorful liquid letters". Dual mechanic
hint without being cluttered.

Color palette:
- Background: Cream + amber gradient
- Tiles: 4 element colors (red, blue, green, gold)
- Letters: White or off-white (high contrast on color tiles)
- Glow: Gold #D4B87A
- Bottle silhouette ghost: Muted warm wood #8B6F47 at 30% opacity

NEVER include:
- Wordle official green/yellow tile colors (avoid trademark issue).
- Multiple word grids (single 4-letter word "BREW" only).
- Tiny details unreadable at 60×60 px.
- Painterly style.
- Anime exaggeration.
- Aggressive monetization markers.

Format: 1024×1024 square, ultra-sharp. Letter grid must be visually
striking at thumbnail scale.
```
