# 📱 App Icon V2 — Character Focused

**Amaç**: App Store / Play Store app icon, varyasyon 2 — Royal Match formülü (mascot karakter face).

**Boyut**: 1024×1024.

**Konsept**: Iris + Mochi merkez. Royal Match'in King Robert karakter formülü.

---

## Sora / ChatGPT / Midjourney Prompt

```
App icon design for POURLE casual mobile puzzle game, square 1024×1024,
smooth 3D toon render Royal Match style. Character-focused mascot icon
(in style of Royal Match's King Robert icon).

Composition: Iris (POV character) centered, bust-up framing showing her
upper torso and head. Mochi (gray tabby cat) sitting on her shoulder,
both looking warmly at viewer. A small glass bottle with colorful
liquid layers visible in lower corner (game asset hint).

Iris details:
- Age 22 female, NATURAL CHESTNUT brown hair in practical bun, light
  freckles, warm green eyes, friendly smile, mass-market neutral
  feminine face (NOT princess, NOT anime).
- Cream tunic + amber apron visible in framing.
- Bright cheerful expression — "come play with us!" energy.
- NEVER purple/lavender/pink hair, NEVER ribbons/bows/hearts.

Mochi details:
- Gray tabby cat (silver gray + dark stripe), green eyes, small gold
  collar bell, sitting on Iris's shoulder, head tilted curiously.
- Warm friendly cat face, NOT anime kawaii uwu.

Background: Warm gradient cream → amber (matching V1 palette). Subtle
soft golden particles floating around. Glass bottle in lower-right
corner showing colorful liquid layers (game hint).

Style: Royal Match smooth 3D toon render. Iris face should be the
strongest read at 60×60 px — clear features, warm welcoming. Royal
Match's icon is famously a King Robert face — POURLE's V2 takes same
formula with Iris.

Color palette:
- Background: Cream #F5E8D0 → amber #D4B87A gradient
- Iris hair: Chestnut #7A4C2D
- Iris skin: Warm medium #E8C9A8
- Iris outfit: Cream + amber accents
- Mochi: Gray #8B8C82 + dark stripe #3A3D38
- Glass bottle: Multi-color liquid (small accent)

NEVER include:
- Iris purple/pink/lavender hair.
- Anime large-eye exaggeration.
- Magical girl outfit (NO ribbons, hearts, bows).
- Princess gown.
- Pin-up framing.
- Painterly style.
- Cluttered details that don't read at 60×60 px.

Format: 1024×1024 square, ultra-sharp, optimized for store thumbnail.
Iris face must be visually punchy at small sizes — clean shapes, high
contrast, warm welcoming expression.
```
